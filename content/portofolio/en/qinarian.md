## Solution

Qinarian gives developers a place to showcase projects, share knowledge, and collaborate. Its case-study gallery, discussion space, and resource library are available in Indonesian and English.

## My Role

As **Fullstack Developer and UI/UX Designer**, I designed and built the platform from scratch, including the interface, content management, bilingual support, and performance optimization.

## Impact and Results

Visitors can browse community projects, read guides, and join discussions in one place. Editors can update content through the content management system I set up.
