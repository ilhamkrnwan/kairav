## Solution

RuangNU brings together information and services that were spread across multiple sites and apps. Nahdlatul Ulama members can find prayer times, worship guides, an Islamic library, and information about events and local chapters.

## My Role

As **Web Developer and Database Designer**, I built the interface and backend, designed the database for content and accounts, and developed the library and worship tools. I worked with the NU community to define feature requirements.

## Impact and Results

The platform gives members one place to find daily worship resources, Islamic educational material, and community information across devices.
