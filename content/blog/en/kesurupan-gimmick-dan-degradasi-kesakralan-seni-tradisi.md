# When Cultural Arts Get Distorted: Protecting Tradition from Gimmicks and Superstition Exploitation

The traditional performing arts of the Indonesian archipelago—such as Barongan, Jaranan, Reog, and Kuda Lumping—represent the pinnacle of **high-level choreographic mastery, polyrhythmic musicality, spiritual depth, and the harmonious alignment between humanity and the cosmos.**

Yet, observe what frequently unfolds across contemporary public stages and viral social media algorithms today: performances that should serve as showcases of intricate choreography and majestic gamelan orchestration are too often degraded into cheap, sensationalist spectacles. Exaggerated, staged trances, dangerous glass-eating stunts for viral engagement, reckless stage incursions that endanger spectators, and youth brawls excused under the guise of "supernatural possession."

This trajectory not only tarnishes the essence of traditional art forms, but also alienates younger generations and cultural enthusiasts from appreciating the profound heritage of their ancestors.

This essay examines why the hijack of traditional culture by sensational opportunists must be critically addressed, and how we can restore traditional performing arts to their rightful aesthetic dignity.

---

## 1. Between Sacred Ecstasy (*Trance Ritual*) and Commercial Gimmicks

In traditional Javanese and Austronesian cosmology, entering a trance state (*ndadi* or spiritual ecstasy) within ancient rites was never a commercial sideshow. It was an integral component of solemn village purification ceremonies (*ruwat bumi*), symbolic communal healing, or the experiential summit of spiritual devotion (*manunggaling roso*), governed by strict ancestral protocols, offerings, and sacred prayers.

What transpires today across numerous street stages is **vulgar desacralization**:
- **Staged Trances Choreographed for Viral Cameras:** Performers simulating violent spasms on cue solely to provoke audience hysteria and maximize short-form video engagement (TikTok / Reels).
- **Unruly Behavior Stripped of Aesthetic Value:** Vandalizing market stalls, damaging public property, or intimidating children in the audience is not cultural expression; it is disorderly conduct masquerading as art.
- **Excusing Brawls with Mystical Justifications:** Inter-group rivalries and personal vendettas are routinely whitewashed under the pretext of *"being overtaken by spirits"*, attempting to evade legal and civic accountability.

---

## 2. The Cultural Fallout: Why Does the Public Grow Apathetic?

The unchecked sensationalism of superstition gimmicks incurs severe cultural costs:

### A. Alienation of Educated and Global Audiences
When profound traditions are reduced to chaotic and hazardous spectacles, creative communities, educated youths, and international audiences naturally retreat. Those who initially sought to study the polyrhythmic genius of gamelan or the intricate woodcarving of ceremonial masks become repelled by stage thuggery.

### B. Overshadowing the Labor of Authentic Masters
Behind the sensational noise work thousands of dedicated traditional masters—drummers who spent decades perfecting intricate syncopation, master carvers preserving sacred teak wood techniques, and visionary choreographers—whose dedication is overshadowed by chaotic troublemakers.

### C. Destruction of Profound Philosophical Messages
The iconic Barongan mask, for example, represents the eternal mastery of primal animalistic instincts through human discipline. When performers indulge in unrestrained chaos that violates ethical boundaries, that noble philosophical symbolism is shattered.

---

## 3. The Path of Restoration: Reclaiming Aesthetic Majesty

How do we revitalize traditional performance art so it commands global respect in the modern era?

```
[Future of Traditional Arts]
├── 1. Rigorous Aesthetic Standards & Choreographic Discipline
├── 2. Deconstructing Mystical Gimmicks ──> High-Level Curation
└── 3. Contemporary Production Quality (Lighting, Acoustics, Storytelling)
```

1. **Re-center Choreographic Precision and Musical Mastery:** Stages must prioritize dance technique, drum dynamics, melodic slendro-pelog harmonies, and expressive theatrical storytelling over sensationalist gimmicks.
2. **Enforce Civic Order and Safety:** Art collectives and community authorities must uphold uncompromising standards. Any disruptive violence or public vandalism must face legal consequences without mystical exemptions.
3. **Elevate Production and Presentation Standards:** Incorporating professional lighting, crystal-clear acoustic engineering, and narrative curation will elevate traditional Indonesian arts to the global prestige enjoyed by Japanese Kabuki or Spanish Flamenco.

---

## Conclusion

Culture is the soul and dignity of a nation. Our traditional arts were bequeathed to us by ancestors not to be turned into sensationalist mockery, excuses for mob violence, or cheap viral commodities.

It is time for art lovers, content creators, cultural practitioners, and the broader public to take a principled stand: **reject destructive sensationalism, and celebrate the authentic technical brilliance, philosophical depth, and sacred dignity of traditional arts.** Only through uncompromising standards will this ancestral heritage thrive and command the admiration of the world.
