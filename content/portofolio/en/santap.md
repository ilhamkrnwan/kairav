## Solution

Santap connects customers, cashiers, kitchen staff, and outlet managers in a single workflow. Customers scan a table QR code to order and pay via QRIS, while cashiers can manage open bills and kitchen staff receive orders to prepare.

## My Role

As **Fullstack Web & Mobile Developer**, I built the web ordering flow, cashier and kitchen apps, and the admin dashboard. I also worked on QRIS payments and the data model for orders, tables, bills, and multiple outlets.

## Impact and Results

The workflow helps staff record orders and track payment status in one place. It supports dine-in and takeaway and is designed for multiple outlets with separate data.
