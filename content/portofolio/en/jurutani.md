## Solution

Jurutani gives Indonesian farmers one place to check weather, commodity prices, and planting guidance. Farmers can also consult experts, ask an AI assistant for advice, get an initial assessment from crop photos, and list harvests on a marketplace.

## My Role

As **Fullstack Web & Android Developer**, I designed and built the web and Android interfaces, implemented the database and services that connect them, and integrated the photo-based AI consultation feature.

## Impact and Results

Farmers can check information before making decisions in the field and offer their harvest directly to buyers. The mobile-first design and offline access support use in areas with limited connectivity.
