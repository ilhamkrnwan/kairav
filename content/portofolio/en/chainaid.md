## Donation prototype on Ethereum Sepolia

ChainAid demonstrates a blockchain-based donation workflow. Organizations can manage campaigns, and donors can trace campaign transactions. The demo runs on Ethereum **Sepolia**, a test network for simulating transactions.

## My contribution

I contributed as a **Backend Developer** and worked on the admin panel for reviewing organizations, approving or rejecting applications, restricting organization access, and exporting PDF reports. Fauzan Arisanto handled the smart contract integration.
