## Campus orientation as a game

GENIUS UNU Jogja 2026 was used during Universitas Nahdlatul Ulama Yogyakarta’s new-student orientation program. Students explored campus facilities in groups through RPG-style quests. The platform tracked challenge progress, digital stamps, and participant rankings. The public site explains how quests work and provides an orientation guide.

## My contribution

I contributed as a **Backend Developer** on the team led by Fauzan Arisanto. The system connected the student experience and admin portal to the backend service.
