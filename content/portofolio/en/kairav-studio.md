## Solution

Kairav Studio needed a site that introduces the studio and shows the quality of its work. The site organizes services, workflows, and case studies so prospective clients can understand what the studio offers before getting in touch.

## My Role

As the **Initiator, Designer, and Web Developer**, I defined the identity and visual direction, planned the site structure, and built the site and its animations. I also optimized images, performance, and content structure for search.

## Impact and Results

The site gives the studio an official place to present its services and project work. Its structure makes it easy to add case studies and services as the studio grows.
