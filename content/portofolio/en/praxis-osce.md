## OSCE simulation platform

Praxis helps medical students and examiners run practice sessions for the **Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE)**. It brings station rotations, synchronized timers, digital rubrics, and score summaries into one workflow.

## My contribution

I contributed to backend development alongside Fauzan Arisanto, who led development and worked on the frontend. Our work supported the simulation workflow from running stations through scoring and reviewing results.
