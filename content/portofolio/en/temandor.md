## Ductwork sketches for site foremen

Temandor helps foremen who know ductwork in the field and are new to CAD software. They can draw a floor plan, lay out ventilation ducts, and hand the sketch to a drafter for review.

## What you can try now

The editor adds elbows at bends and flags unconnected duct ends. Users can export a layered DXF for CAD review and download a CSV material list for a preliminary estimate.

## My contribution

I developed the web editor prototype and its workflow, from placing components to checking and exporting the sketch. You can try the prototype with a sample office floor plan.
